# KnowGacha — Privacy Policy

[日本語](privacy-ja.md)

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Operator: KnowGacha / akmrbaby

KnowGacha is a learning app for discovering concepts outside your usual fields. This policy explains how information is stored, transmitted and used when you use the app. It is operated by its developer, akmrbaby.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The app stores discovered topics and dates, records needed to calculate daily draw credits and their start date, welcome draws, language, reminder and badge settings, and your Map position on your device. These records support draw balances, revisiting discoveries, display preferences and notifications. Evaluation builds may also store a development date setting.

The app has no user accounts or feature that synchronizes your discovery history to a server operated by the developer. Device storage may be included in operating-system backups, subject to your OS and backup-service settings.

## 2. App update communications

We use Expo EAS Update to deliver fixes and updates to content and images. The app may check for updates and download update files when it starts or at other update-check times.

The receiving services receive connection information such as your IP address. Requests may also include an Expo client identifier, platform, app runtime version, identifiers for current, embedded or failed updates, and technical error information such as launch failures. The client identifier is separate from an advertising identifier.

This information supports update delivery, compatibility checks and troubleshooting. We have not implemented a feature that attaches your discovery history or notification preferences to update requests. Please consult the providers' privacy policies for their processing and retention practices.

[Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

## 3. Notifications and app icon badges

Reminders and badge updates are local notifications scheduled on your device with OS permission. Scheduling uses notification times, discovery status and remaining draws. The app does not obtain and send remote push tokens to the developer or Expo's push delivery service.

You can change reminders and badges independently in the app and revoke permission in your device notification settings. Declining notifications does not prevent you from opening discoveries, reading or using the Map.

## 4. Contact and TestFlight feedback

You may voluntarily contact knowgacha.dev@gmail.com about bugs, feedback or privacy questions. Email contact provides us with your sender address, message and any images or other files you choose to attach. This information is used to respond, investigate issues and improve the app. The app does not automatically attach your discovery history or device identifiers to a contact request.

We use Google's Gmail to receive and manage support email. Please also consult Google's Privacy Policy for its handling of information involved in sending and receiving email.

Posts to the current GitHub channel are public. Do not include your name, email address, device identifiers, discovery history or other personal or private information. Public posts follow GitHub's retention and deletion mechanisms; copies made by others may remain after deletion.

If you use TestFlight, you can contact the developer through Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to feedback, crashes and usage diagnostics handled by TestFlight. Review any text and images before submitting them.

## 5. External services and links

Opening references, the public policy, GitHub support or Apple and Expo documents takes you to an external browser or service. Those services' policies govern connection information and actions on their sites. The public policy is hosted on GitHub. We use Gmail to receive support email.

The current app has no advertising, advertising tracking or dedicated behavioral analytics SDK. It has no feature that accesses your location, contacts or photo-library contents.

[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 6. Retention, deletion and settings

History and settings remain in device storage while needed to use the app. You can delete local data through OS app-data deletion or by deleting the app. On iOS, Offload App keeps data; choose Delete App to remove its data. Manage data in OS backups separately through your backup settings.

Deleting the app removes access to locally saved discovery history and draw records. The app has no account or server synchronization for remotely viewing or deleting that history. Retention and deletion of information handled by external update services, TestFlight or other providers follow those providers' policies.

We retain support email for as long as needed to respond and review related issues, and delete information when no longer needed, subject to any legally required retention. Contact knowgacha.dev@gmail.com about reviewing or deleting contact information or other privacy requests. Do not include personal information in public GitHub posts.

[Email us about privacy](mailto:knowgacha.dev@gmail.com)

## 7. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its revision date when features or information practices change. Material changes will be communicated in the app or on the public page. Where a new permission or consent is needed, we will request it appropriately.
